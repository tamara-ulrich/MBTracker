# Privacy Policy — MBTracker

*Last updated: May 2026*

MBTracker is a personal study tracking app for Mandarin Blueprint learners.

## Data Collection
MBTracker does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
All data you enter (study sessions, progress, settings) is stored
exclusively on your device using Apple's built-in storage (SwiftData
and UserDefaults).

## Third Parties
MBTracker does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or
tracking services.

## Data Deletion
All app data can be deleted at any time via Settings → Clear All Data,
or by uninstalling the app.

## Disclaimer
This app is provided as-is, without warranty or support of any kind.
Use at your own risk.
